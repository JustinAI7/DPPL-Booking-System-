Department of Physical Projects & Logistics

Department Objective

To elevate the standard of event execution within UNORSSG by replacing archaic, paper-driven workflows with a zero-typing digital infrastructure. Our objective is to minimize administrative turnaround times, eliminate scheduling friction, and provide a seamless, click-and-select request portal that optimizes the organization and execution of bookings, permits, and logistical assets for the student body.
